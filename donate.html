Corporate Partnership Opportunities

We welcome corporate sponsorships to support Blood Cancer United's critical work in:

  • Funding Research: Support innovative research projects working toward cures for leukemia, lymphoma, and myeloma
  • Supporting Patients: Provide resources and support services for patients and families affected by blood cancer
  • Accelerating Cures: Help advance promising treatments from the lab to clinical trials
  • Building Hope: Create a community dedicated to ending blood cancer

100% of funds raised go directly to Blood Cancer United's mission. As Student Visionaries of the Year, we volunteer our time to ensure every dollar makes maximum impact.
