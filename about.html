About Mission Remission

Meet the team accelerating cures for blood cancer

Our Why

We can't change the stories that have already ended, but we can help change the stories that are still being written.

Mission Remission is more than a fundraising campaign—it's our commitment to making a real difference in the fight against blood cancer. As Student Visionaries of the Year, we're dedicating ourselves to supporting Blood Cancer United's mission of accelerating cures through cutting-edge research and patient support programs.

Blood cancer affects thousands of families every year, and we believe that through collective action, awareness, and dedicated fundraising, we can contribute to breakthroughs that save lives. Our goal is not just to raise funds, but to build a community of advocates who refuse to accept blood cancer as inevitable.

About Blood Cancer United

Blood Cancer United is at the forefront of funding innovative research to accelerate cures for blood cancers including leukemia, lymphoma, and myeloma. Through their work, they:

  • Fund groundbreaking research projects nationwide
  • Support patients and families affected by blood cancer
  • Advocate for increased awareness and resources
  • Build a community dedicated to finding cures

As Student Visionaries of the Year, 100% of the funds we raise go directly to Blood Cancer United's mission.
