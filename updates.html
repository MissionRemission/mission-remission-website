October 28, 2026

Mission Remission Campaign Launch!

We're thrilled to announce the official launch of Mission Remission, our Student Visionaries of the Year campaign supporting Blood Cancer United. Our team—Dheekshitha Kota, Saashi Shailesh, and Laura Sofia Caballero—is committed to raising funds for life-saving blood cancer research.

Our goal is to raise $10,000 to support Blood Cancer United's mission of accelerating cures. Every dollar raised goes directly to funding innovative research projects and patient support programs.

Stay tuned for upcoming events, fundraising milestones, and ways you can get involved. Together, we can make a difference in the fight against blood cancer.
